One thing I wish I do more is write. But for some reason there's always some blocker, some reason or little bump that I look at and go "Neeehhh". 

I got this blog back up and running. I was previously on Bear blog, which I like the ethos of, but for some reason made me want to write less. Not sure why, maybe because I knew I wasn't just writing into the aether, but to a platform.

Here I write for myself, no need for upvotes or [vanity metrics](/blog/_posts/2023-01-09-vanity-metrics.md). 

So, I plan on writing more. Also, the 100 day challenge I gave for myself previously felt like pressure and if I missed a day, I then felt less inclined to write again. 

Here I'm going to just write into the void, when I want and what I want. 

Feels good.
