I like building things that help others and that are fun to use.

Here are a few things that I have built:
* [Daily Tokki](https://dailytokki.com?ref=bearblog) - daily personalized Korean/English language learning right in your inbox
* [Linka](https://linka.cool) - kinda like linktree, but with folders
* [Whiteboard](https://whiteboard.lasky.io) - whiteboard that writes like a human
* [Resgen](https://resgen.app) - tailor resumes to job descriptions
* [highlight.cool](https://highlight.cool) - add Medium-style highlights to your own blog

I also organize a 6-week program called LMT2 ([Let's Make Things Together](https://blog.tempproject.dev/lets-make-things-together/)) where we build a ton of cool stuff.
